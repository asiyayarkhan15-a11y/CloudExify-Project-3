The practice

We built the clinic we could not find

Aurelia began in 2011 in two rented rooms above a pharmacy on Main Boulevard. Dr. Sana Aurangzeb had spent six years in a high-volume hospital department where a molar root canal was allotted twenty-two minutes, and had watched good clinicians become fast ones.

So the first rule written on the wall here was a scheduling rule, not a clinical one: no appointment shorter than the treatment deserves. Everything else — the microscopes, the CBCT scanner, the ceramist we fly in from Karachi twice a month — followed from having the time to use them properly.

Unhurried

90-minute slots as standard. We do not double-book chairs.

Transparent

Every quote itemised. If a tooth can be watched, we say so.

Accountable

We re-do our own work free within the warranty period.
